Welcome

A peaceful haven in Namugongo

Whether you're travelling for a spiritual pilgrimage, a family getaway, or a quiet retreat from the city, Egret Pilgrims Inn offers the perfect blend of modern comfort and serene hospitality.

Nestled within lush, landscaped gardens along the Naalya–Namugongo Road, our inn is a calm, restful sanctuary just a short walk from the historic Uganda Martyrs Catholic Shrine and Museum.
